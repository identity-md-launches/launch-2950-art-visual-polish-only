        How Wheelbound works

        Squeektoshi runs on a real wheel. Squeekchain measures every turn, settles the work of each 10-minute round on chain, and publishes the measurements at api.squeekchain.com. Wheelbound reads that public data and turns it into a journey.

        The distance on the trail is the real distance the wheel turned. A landmark sits every 5 km and the land changes every 25 km. Each wheel day (12:00 to 12:00 UTC, as Squeekchain counts it) becomes a chapter: its painting is drawn from the 48 half-hours of running that day, and its text from the settled rounds, the milestones and the records. Each week a boss waits on the trail; the distance run that week is the damage dealt.

        Names, paintings and words are derived from the chain data with a fixed seed, so everyone who opens Wheelbound reads the same story. Add #overlay to the address for a transparent stream overlay.
